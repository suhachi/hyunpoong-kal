## 📊 생성된 인덱스 목록

### 1. support_sessions 컬렉션 (2개)
- ✅ `open` (ASC) + `lastAt` (DESC)
- ✅ `assignedTo` (ASC) + `lastAt` (DESC)

### 2. menus 컬렉션 (4개)
- ✅ `category` (ASC) + `order` (ASC) + `createdAt` (ASC)
- ✅ `category` (ASC) + `name` (ASC) + `createdAt` (ASC)
- ✅ `category` (ASC) + `price` (ASC) + `createdAt` (ASC)
- ✅ `category` (ASC) + `price` (DESC) + `createdAt` (ASC)

### 3. reviews 컬렉션 (3개)
- ✅ `isVisible` (ASC) + `rating` (DESC) + `createdAt` (DESC)
- ✅ `isVisible` (ASC) + `rating` (ASC) + `createdAt` (DESC)
- ✅ `isVisible` (ASC) + `createdAt` (DESC)

### 4. support_messages 컬렉션 (1개)
- ✅ `sessionId` (ASC) + `at` (ASC)
